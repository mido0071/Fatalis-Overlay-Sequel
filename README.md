# Monster Hunter: World — Fatalis Fight Companion (MHW Fatalis Overlay)

Welcome to the **Fatalis Fight Companion**, a specialized, feature-rich overlay tool designed exclusively for *Monster Hunter: World — Iceborne*'s ultimate encounter: the black dragon Fatalis. This repository is not just another UI add-on; it is a meticulously crafted data visualization engine that transforms raw game telemetry into a live, actionable command center. Built by hunters, for hunters, this overlay aims to remove the guesswork from the most punishing fight in the New World, giving you the clarity to focus on what truly matters: your positioning, your timing, and your execution.

Instead of overwhelming you with static numbers, this project offers a dynamic, responsive, and entirely customizable interface that sits elegantly alongside your game window. Think of it as a co-pilot that never blinks, never panics, and always has the exact information you need—from precise HP thresholds to the ever-shifting phase transitions. This is the companion you take into the Secluded Valley when you intend to walk out with 5,000 years of elder dragon history on your back.

This project is built on a philosophy of "clarity through precision." We do not just dump data on your screen; we curate it, contextualize it, and present it in a way that feels like a native extension of your hunter's instincts. Whether you are a speedrunner chasing a sub-10-minute kill or a casual hunter attempting your first solo clear, the Fatalis Fight Companion adapts to your workflow, not the other way around.

## 📊 Overview: Why This Exists

Fighting Fatalis is less of a monster hunt and more of a multi-phase siege. It involves weapon constraints, environmental hazards, a short 30-minute timer, and a power creep that demands near-perfect play. Standard in-game UI is simply insufficient for this level of complexity. You need to know exactly when the Dragonator is ready, when the roaming ballista becomes available, and precisely how much health remains in the head break threshold.

## ✨ Core Features: The Arsenal

This overlay is packed with functionalities designed to cover every aspect of the Fatalis encounter. Here is what awaits you inside:

![Features](https://img.shields.io/badge/features-advanced-blueviolet)

- **Live Phase Tracker** 🎯: Automatically detects and displays the current phase (Phase 1, Phase 2, or Phase 3) with visual transitions and color changes. No more guessing if the "blue flame" state is active.
- **Health & Threshold Metrics** : A real-time display of the monster's total HP, the specific HP percentage, and crucially, the remaining HP until the next part break or phase trigger.
- **Head Break Optimizer** 💡: A dedicated module that tracks head damage separately, providing a progress bar toward the crucial second head break, which is essential for maximizing your damage windows.
- **Tactical Cooldown Master** ⏱️: Accurate timers for the Dragonator, the roaming ballista, the one-shot binder, and the smoke bomb timing windows. It counts down to the second, ensuring you never miss a tactical opportunity.
- **Damage Meter & DPS Graph** 📈: Tracks your party's total damage output and individual DPS in a clean, non-intrusive sidebar. Review your performance after the hunt with a detailed breakdown.
- **Environmental Awareness HUD** 🧭: Displays the status of the gate, the falling rocks, and the cannon placement recommendations based on your current position and the phase.
- **Customizable Alert System** 🔔: Set custom sound or visual alerts for specific thresholds (e.g., 5% HP remaining, or when the Dragonator is ready). Choose from a variety of alert presets or create your own.

## 🛠️ Configuration & Customization

![Customization](https://img.shields.io/badge/customization-infinite-green)

Every hunter has a unique playstyle, and our overlay respects that. The `config.json` file, located in the installation directory, offers granular control over every element of the interface. You can adjust the opacity of panels, re-map keybindings for quick toggles, change color schemes for colorblind accessibility, and even define custom damage threshold integers for the alert system.

For those who want to dive deeper, our built-in **Theme Editor** allows you to import community-created themes or craft your own from scratch. The visual style ranges from minimalist monochrome to vivid, high-contrast layouts. The choice is entirely yours. We provide a live preview window in the editor so you can see your changes instantly without relaunching the overlay.

## 🧩 Troubleshooting & FAQ

**Q: The overlay is not detecting my game.**
A: Ensure you are running the game in **Borderless Window** or **Windowed** mode. Fullscreen exclusive mode often blocks overlay rendering. Restart the companion app after switching modes.

**Q: The font is too small on my 4K monitor.**
A: Navigate to the *Display* section in the configuration file and adjust the "Global Scaling" factor from `1.0` to `1.5` or `2.0`. The layout will re-flow automatically to maintain proportionality.

**Q: Can I use this with controller-only input?**
A: Absolutely. The overlay is entirely mouse-independent for its critical functions. Default keybindings include controller chord combinations (e.g., Hold Start + Press X) to toggle different panels.
